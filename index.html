Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow SYSTEM may handle information when used on Discord servers.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server administrator, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message content needed for enabled commands or moderation, and configuration settings.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate commands, manage server settings, provide moderation and logging features, maintain levels or other enabled features, and prevent abuse.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Some information may be stored to provide persistent features. The types of stored information, retention periods, and deletion procedures depend on the bot's actual implementation. Contact the support team for data-related requests.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord or infrastructure providers as needed to operate the bot, or disclosed where required by law.

5. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information and access credentials. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies. Do not use the bot in a way that violates those requirements.

7. Your Requests

You may contact the support team to ask about information associated with your use of the bot or request deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the bot's features and data practices change. The latest version will be published here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Shadow SYSTEM support team through its official support server.
